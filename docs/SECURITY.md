# Security model and invariants

**Status: development candidate, not approval for deployment or real funds.** The $100 activation, 80/10/5/5 allocation, equal collective membership, VRF quota reveal and separate royalties have [source-bound internal verification](../reports/nft-rights-audit/VERIFICATION.md). The subsequent permanent-capacity repair has [separate targeted evidence](../reports/architecture-evolution/VERIFICATION.md). Earlier reports retain their recorded revisions; old passing counts, bytecode sizes and analyzer dispositions do not automatically verify later source changes or live services.

## Assets and authorities

The Vault controls shared physical LP positions and attributed idle principal. Each NFT holds nonredeemable shares of one canonical bucket. Fees holds collected revenue and segregated compound, buyback and remote-reward budgets; Rewards holds funded LP claim liabilities. Immutable per-bucket inboxes hold pending creator payments separately. The NFT owns a distinct immutable royalty receiver and read-only metadata renderer. Royalties and mint proceeds are separate from LP fees and principal.

Governance selects pools, feeds, operators and routes through the configured timelock. Sealing fixes module bindings and the transport endpoint. New append-only bridge policies/routes and bounded operating parameters remain governance trust powers. Guardians can pause strategy operations, not unpause or redeem principal. Keepers maintain approved buckets and have no redemption right. The NFT's coordinator and artwork base cannot be replaced after sealing; governance may admit bounded royalty assets through its NFT wrapper, subject to independent token review.

Future destinations require review of custody code, upgrade/proxy powers, assets and provider semantics. A timelock provides notice rather than proof that remote custody preserves principal. Fixed local modules cannot make an unsafe approved destination safe.

## Intended invariants

1. No owner, keeper or administrator principal-redemption entrypoint, arbitrary Vault call, delegatecall, module replacement or arbitrary LP-NFT transfer exists.
2. Exactly 0.05 ETH mints one dormant canonical NFT, without LP or reward eligibility. The immutable mint recipient cannot be the NFT or its royalty receiver. Activation keeps that NFT and requires at least $100 of original gross source value, with at least 99% deployed as marginal LP and network gas separate. Later contributions retain the 1% execution/undeployed limit. Incumbent assets cannot satisfy an entrant's minimum. Market losses never revoke an activation already completed.
3. At most 1,000 NFTs can be minted. Revealing all of them consumes exactly 600 Commun, 300 Rare and 100 Legendary allocations. One fixed VRF request supplies two words per mint. Only the configured coordinator can fulfill a known request once, and permissionless finalization consumes quotas strictly in mint order. Transfers cannot change a request, ordinal, rarity or color. There is no cancellation, reroll, manual assignment or replacement coordinator.
4. Idle principal is attributed to a shared bucket and aggregate custody ledger. Donations do not create shares. After liquidity placement, at least 95% of validated principal value is deployed; this execution-time check is not a continuous guarantee.
5. Only collected trading fees and processed creator receipts enter 80% owner / 10% collective / 5% compound / 5% buyback accounting. Deposits and LP-removal proceeds are principal. LP owner rewards follow bucket shares; collective rewards give one equal share to each activated canonical NFT, independent of LP size, rarity, age or artwork evolution. Top-ups, transfers and migration cannot create additional membership.
6. Fee collection occurs before share changes and after same-pool entry swaps. Published indexes are checkpointed before new shares or membership become effective. Fractions and unclaimed balances follow the NFT and current ownership.
7. Exact LP allocation uses twenty-raw-unit batches with carried remainder. Funded canonical dust is settled to incumbent owner indexes before entry. The global barrier scans at most 4 historical local buckets, so at most 4 home creator receivers are reachable despite the factory's separate 16-receiver ceiling. The former 16-bucket combined funded workload exceeded the unchanged 12M local gas budget. Actual Entry/venue/token costs still require qualification at the reduced bound. It is not one upkeep operation per NFT. Satellite dust remains funded in its remote-individual budget until authenticated delivery home.
8. Pending creator receipts and funded dust settle under incumbent weights before entry, after entry balancing and after the final LP placement. Late compound value belongs to incumbent NAV. Final deployment, pending-receipt and dust barriers precede share issuance and activation. Already indexed or locally pending rewards cannot be rewritten by an entrant.
9. Unharvested venue fees and undelivered remote rewards follow the existing collection/delivery-time collective membership policy. They are not a promise of historical earned-at-venue membership. Creator receipts use processing-time membership. These timing choices and entry incentives require explicit economic review.
10. NFT-sale royalties have a separate funded ledger: a fixed 5% ERC-2981 payment request goes entirely to the royalty receiver. Activated Commun/Rare/Legendary weights are 1/2/3; Dormant weight is zero. Pending received royalties settle before a new eligible NFT joins. These weights never modify LP fees or collective membership. ERC-2981 does not enforce marketplace payment.
11. Royalty eligibility persists through price losses and migration. Artwork uses current marked principal/idle/compound NAV proportional to shares, can move down across $1,000/$10,000 thresholds, and reports valuation unavailable during migration or dependency outages. Metadata does not authorize claims, change financial weights or block migration. Claims do not require a metadata oracle read.
12. Claims consume funded entitlement before transfer and require current NFT ownership. Strategy pause does not disable funded LP or royalty claims. There is no administrator sweep of principal, reward liabilities, royalty prefunds or unsupported assets.
13. Transfers use exact amounts and destinations; temporary approvals are reset. Exact token deltas reject ordinary transfer taxes. Oracle freshness, decimals, reference price, deviation, output bounds and deadlines are separate execution checks.
14. Version-3 bridge messages authenticate route and origin, preserve whole-bucket shares and canonical token provenance, consume each origin nonce once and require exact backing. Remote token identities cannot be rebound for a bucket. No custody hop remints the NFT or changes canonical membership/rarity rights.
15. Failed destination LP placement preserves attributed principal for retry. Source-completion ACKs match frozen departures; canonical location ACKs require the next epoch and previous custodian. Historical retries cannot regress location. Timeouts alone do not authorize refunds or prove cancellation.
16. Legacy remote rewards use authenticated historical vault/token paths even after principal moves. Creator payments cannot satisfy missing authenticated remote liabilities. Home compound reserves while away await compatible maintenance after return.
17. Off-chain services are not custody authorities. Indexer projections can be rebuilt; ambiguous keeper broadcasts halt resubmission until transaction identity is resolved.

## Trust, liveness and limits

No implementation guarantees original token quantities, USD value, yield, honest feeds, bridge solvency, chain availability or safe migration to arbitrary code. Malicious or compromised governance, feeds, tokens, future destinations and providers can break the economic intent without a local withdrawal function. Immutability also makes errors hard or impossible to repair.

No production VRF coordinator, funded subscription or actual selected-chain fulfillment has been established. Configuring a compatible ABI is not proof of verifiable randomness. An unfulfilled earlier request blocks later reveals, and therefore their activation; no fallback/reroll exists. Review coordinator authenticity, finality, subscription backing, callback gas and failure recovery before any paid mint.

An admitted creator-payment token/wrapper can block global entry settlement; an admitted royalty asset can block royalty synchronization and new eligibility. Positive rebases cannot be distinguished from raw donations, negative rebases fail closed, and taxed claims revert. Review code, proxy/blacklist powers, decimals and economic dust value rather than trusting a single successful transfer. Unsupported assets, constructor prefunds and forced ETH without an admitted LP wrapper have no rescue. The separate royalty receiver accepts native ETH directly.

The transport boundary is not a production bridge. Equivalent-asset migration preserves identical nominal units rather than converting unrelated assets. Canonical NFTs never move chains; satellite holder top-ups remain absent. The candidate includes a strictly pinned native-ETH/Pons adapter, with actual-venue fork tests using mock authorities/VRF and synthetic USD calibration. That is not production venue/route qualification. Creator receipts do not establish a guaranteed Pons revenue entitlement. Actual VRF, feeds, routes, wrapper authority, bridge providers, staged operation and independent audit/remediation remain release gates. Destination reservations now consume permanent capacity idempotently, including unfunded None-state identities. Real bridging remains disabled pending independent admission, remote backing and operating review. The source does not authenticate a remote reservation proof; see [architecture review](../reports/architecture-evolution/REVIEW.md).

## Secrets and operations

Keep production keys, access tokens and passwords outside source, documentation and Git. Keeper signing uses an external encrypted `cast` keystore and private password file, a dedicated minimally funded keeper account and reviewed transaction limits. RPC observations and confirmations are not cryptographic protection against a malicious RPC.

Do not expose test coordinators, bridge mocks, bootstrap configuration or simulation RPCs to users. The local deployment script rejects public-chain IDs and does not seal the system. Read [operations](OPERATIONS.md), [NFT rules](NFT_METADATA.md) and the [release approval record](RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) before preparing a deployment.
