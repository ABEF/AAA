# ETH/ZZZ routing boundaries

Current routing/asset controls are consolidated into [Direct activation Entry](ACTIVATION_ENTRY.md#routing-controls), with [wallet review](ACTIVATION_ENTRY.md#profiles-and-wallet-review). Entry3 forwards ETH/fixedZZZ; Vault balancing and separate earned-reward conversions retain their own reviewed venues. No Entry route setter or conversion child exists.
