## Identity and custody

Only the canonical chain issues NFTs. Minting forwards the charge separately, requests verifiable randomness and creates a dormant ERC-721 with no LP or rewards. Mint and payment/request failures roll back atomically. The NFT enforces the1,000cap and excludes royalty/mint-receiver collisions. Before canonical sealing, the randomness configuration must exist; actual provider availability remains a deployment gate.

Each approved pool/strategy has one bucket and one physical LP NFT. Bucket identity is keccak(homeChain,homeVault,initialPoolId); each economic NFT has its own canonical identity, bucket association and nonredeemable shares. `group(bucketId)` exposes custody; `nftBucket`, `sharesOf`, `contributionUSD` expose NFT rights/history. Never sum one physical LP once per NFT. `contributionUSD` is not a current valuation or reward weight.

`poolBucket` permanently reserves venues for their buckets. Governance reviews destination capacity before a move and reserves an external-custody return destination when required by that workflow. Distinct buckets do not merge. NFT transfers change the current claimant without changing shares, membership, rarity or accrued balances. Operators cannot claim as owners; holders cannot redeem principal. Separately approved external custody is a governance trust described below.

## Entry and membership

Activation requires revealed traits and at least $100 original gross source value, with at least 99% actually deployed as marginal oracle-valued LP, excluding incumbent idle/compound value. The immutable Registry-created [Entry](ACTIVATION_ENTRY.md) accepts admitted direct pair assets, exact native ETH forwarding for the selected native pool, or reviewed typed single-hop V3 conversion into one accounting currency. It binds current ownership, exact Vault calldata and original measured source USD through a callback-safe context. Separate conversion and LP legs cannot each consume a new 1% allowance. Actual routes, liquidity and feeds need qualification; arbitrary calldata/conversion is not supported. The same NFT may receive permanent additions from $5 in original gross source value at current share NAV, with the same aggregate bound; a higher configured pool minimum still applies. After placement no more than 5% measured principal NAV may remain idle. Pool limits, fresh valuation, minimum liquidity and deadline guards remain.

Before share/membership changes, the vault harvests earlier fees and synchronizes all bounded registered creator inboxes. Sub-twenty-unit dust settles to existing owner shares. Entry swaps are followed by settlement so their fees belong to incumbents; old LP is revalued after balancing. Fresh5%compounding belongs to incumbent NAV. Final LP updates, late receipts,95%deployment and pending/dust guards precede owner checkpoint and share issuance.

First shares equal measured net principal USD; later shares round down as `supply * netNewUSD / (incumbentNAV + historicalCompoundValue)`. Historical non-current compounding stays incumbent backing and cannot subsidize the new contribution's 99% deployed-LP floor. Zero-NAV/zero-share/cap violations revert. Donations, unclaimed rewards and buyback reserves do not buy shares. After successful issuance the vault activates one `ZZZMembership` share for an initially dormant NFT and notifies the fixed NFT, which joins its separate rarity royalty ledger. All failures roll back entry atomically. A top-up never adds collective/royalty weight.

## Funded LP rewards

Twenty raw units split16owner/2collective/1compound/1buyback. Owner indexes are pool/token-specific and use nonredeemable shares. Collective indexes are token-wide and give each activated canonical NFT one equal share. Eligibility persists through price declines, transfers and migration. Before the one-time membership is enabled, all published indexes are checkpointed. No Power module or USD reward weighting remains.

At most 32 admitted ERC20 LP assets plus the native ETH settlement asset bound checkpoint loops; native ETH now supports indexed venue income alongside already converted credits, without counting conversion as new income. Fractions carry per NFT; global division dust stays reserved. Smaller receipts carry and the maximum19raw-unit membership dust exception requires asset-specific economic review. No administrator can withdraw reward, orphan, compound or principal balances outside their constrained purpose. Claims remain available during operational pause.

The selected pool's actual earned venue fees fund this allocation when harvested; Uniswap's protocol fee is not PoPL revenue. Optional external payments enter dedicated immutable per-bucket receivers and actual processed funding uses the same allocation. No Pons creator agreement is required for the selected hookless pool. At most four receivers are reachable under the current Vault capacity, and they are globally settled before entry; the factory retains a separate defensive ceiling of 16. Receipt timing uses processing-time weights, without a promised creator percentage or retrospective entitlement. Home external funding never pays authenticated remote obligations. [External rewards](EXTERNAL_REWARDS.md).

## NFT royalties and metadata

The NFT owns immutable royalty/metadata helpers. ERC-2981 requests5%sale royalties payable directly to the separate receiver;100%of eligible receipts is reserved for activated rarity weights1/2/3. NativeETH and up to16reviewed ERC20assets use funded indexes, pre-join pending synchronization and current-owner claims. There is no LP revenue split or arbitrary administrator sweep in this ledger. Its separately approved paused per-currency custody exit preserves liabilities and explicitly trusts the recipient; it does not settle or erase holder rights. Pre-admission/constructor/zero-eligibility funds are quarantined.

VRF callbacks only store request-bound words. Permissionless reveal follows mint order, drawing without replacement to exact 600/300/100 quotas. A separate word draws a fixed artwork entry without replacement within that rarity. Eight base colors form 58 palettes, combined with four patterns: Flowing arcs, Segmented panels, Orbital rings and Twin circuit routes. Rarity, palette and pattern remain immutable; palette/pattern do not affect rewards, while rarity sets only the separate royalty weight. The 1,000-entry inventory has 200 monochrome, 500 bicolor and 300 tricolor slots, each with the 60/30/10 rarity mix; identical palette/pattern/rarity finishes occur at most four times and all 100 Legendary looks are unique. Missing earlier fulfillment blocks later reveal; no reroll/cancellation bypass exists. [NFT details](NFT_METADATA.md).

The renderer selects among 2,784 supported palette/pattern/rarity/evolution SVG states, separate from the NFT supply. It values current home-chain LP amounts, attributed idle and compound budgets using approved oracles, multiplied by shares/supply. Enhanced starts at $1,000 and Singularity at $10,000; market losses can downgrade artwork. Remote/in-flight/stale valuation is explicitly unavailable, with labelled fallback art. Metadata never gates claims/migration. Reveal/contributions emit ERC-4906 notifications. Permissionless per-token and bounded ordinal-range calls notify actual hashed IDs without price reads. An optional confirmed-block watcher uses the existing keeper's serial budgeted transaction path; permanent content publication, staged watcher operation and marketplace cache integration remain production work.

## Maintenance and migration

Keeper collect/rebalance/compound operations run once per bucket, with no NFT-holder loop. They retain trigger, cooldown, oracle/spot, range, swap, minimum-liquidity and deadline guards. [Approved range changes](RANGE_POLICY_UPDATES.md) can trigger ordinary rebalance at a flat price after cooldown; comparison at the recorded center prevents price-drift churn. Pooling does not remove impermanent loss, swap costs or MEV; real strategy/gas economics remain unapproved.

`Migration.moveFunds` atomically closes and deploys actual LP in another admitted pool with the same ordered accounting pair. Native V2 preserves actual ETH identity and supports same-native-pair fee-tier changes. ETH/WETH atomic conversion is unfinished and rejected; WETH is a distinct historical/optional asset. One fixed starting-price 3% aggregate loss floor covers the complete move, with gas separate; NFT shares and original home identity remain unchanged. Destination contributions and conversion routes are switched deliberately after verifying the resulting LP.

[Governed custody handoff](GOVERNED_CUSTODY_HANDOFF.md) replaces the removed v3 bridge/pair execution stack. Governance approves an exact request and pauses globally before `exit`. Normal exit converts only attributed principal into configured ZZZ under stricter swap minima and the aggregate 3% floor. A separately approved `rawExit` bypasses swaps/USD valuation and sends both current accounting assets under explicit raw minima. It is never selected automatically after normal failure. Native V2 raw proceeds are ETH/ZZZ without wrapper calls; venue/token/runtime failures can still prevent execution.

An approved recipient can be an EOA or contract and may bridge or migrate tokens externally. Once principal leaves, that recipient can lose or divert it; governance explicitly trusts its custody and liveness, and the source cannot compel return. NFT shares and LP/royalty liabilities stay recorded. Local completion requires actual destination LP, recovery requires the full actual sent assets, and normal completion retains its original 97% fixed-price floor. Cross-chain verification/return requires pinned finality and authenticated closure proofs with no multisig/timeout override; no real verifier/provider/remote ledger is supplied. New contributions remain blocked while the principal plan/custody is outstanding.

Holder rewards, royalties and compound/buyback reserves are separate domains and are not swept by principal exit. Royalty currencies have their own approved full-balance custody/return path preserving liabilities. Historical remote counters and already-allocated rewards retain their original funding barriers; the retired bridge selectors cannot be re-enabled by binding new addresses in the current controller.

[Parking](CUSTODY_PARKING.md) uses custody status 6 without changing old enum values. While globally paused, a governance-approved Migration request binds exact custody/shares/LP, nonce, epoch, ordered currencies and raw principal-close minimums. Vault harvests venue fees separately, closes into attributed idle and keeps all principal. This path omits source USD and creator-inbox reads; current venue/runtime/token checks remain, without a wrapper dependency for native pools. Keeper maintenance stops while Parked, but funded LP/royalty claims, NFT transfers and eligibility persist. Resume uses the same ordered pair and fresh ordinary deployment guards. A separately approved raw external exit may leave Parked custody without USD pricing; normal conversion/completion still requires its valuation and loss guards.

## Release and trust boundaries

The current core has **17 contracts: the controller and 16 required sealed modules**, including Membership and the four canonical handoff modules. **Nine automatically created helpers/factories**, optional converters and any later per-bucket external inboxes also require [inventory](DEPLOYMENT_ADDRESSES.md). The old bridge/receiver/routes/transport IDs cannot be bound. Sealing fixes core bindings and verifies canonical companion owners. Governance retains constrained immediate configuration and approved custody powers, guardians pause operations and keepers execute bounded upkeep. Governance approval cannot prove an arbitrary external recipient or future destination preserves principal.

The [same-chain canonical handoff](CANONICAL_HANDOFF.md) freezes ownership and accounting, imports exact state and classified backing, and atomically retires the source/activates one replacement. It supports exact abort restoration before retirement and late funding afterward. Native V2 uses `ZZZ_CANONICAL_SAME_CHAIN_NATIVE_V2`; legacy V1 import is rejected. Funds transfer before zero-value acknowledgment, with separate Vault, Fees, Rewards and royalty backing. It requires revealed mints, parked local custody, satisfied legacy accounting barriers and no outstanding principal/royalty custody. Future-chain proof/transport and lineage support remain pre-seal work; these requirements are distinct from moving LP custody alone.

The LP adapter supports conventional hookless static-fee ERC20 Uniswap v4 pools and initialized static, hookless native ETH/ZZZ fee tiers on the pinned Robinhood deployment. The selected pool has a static 1.002% LP fee and an observed approximately 1.101% combined swap fee; only actual earned LP fees are PoPL revenue. Native V2 preserves the real PoolKey and actual ETH custody/accounting/exit identity. Other native pairs, dynamic native fees and hooks are rejected.

[Native ETH custody/accounting/recovery](NATIVE_ETH_ARCHITECTURE.md) is the requested pre-seal architecture direction, with WETH limited to reviewed operations that actually need it; the native V2 implementation has [scoped local evidence](../reports/native-eth-port/VERIFICATION.md), while explicit ETH/WETH conversion, old-schema import and production qualification remain open. Typed v4 conversion support and bounded post-seal operating extensions also remain unfinished. ZZZ reward conversion retains its 1% maximum loss, gas separate, and requires a cheaper qualified route; the selected pool is not qualified for full conversion at reference prices. Production inventory, real input/conversion routes, proof-verifying VRF, independent pricing, authorities, marketplace/service rehearsals and independent audit/remediation remain open. Public contracts are undeployed and production is NOT APPROVED.
