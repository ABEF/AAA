## Off-chain operation and integration boundaries

The keeper discovers buckets and performs maintenance once per physical LP group, rather than once per NFT. Entry settlement additionally scans bounded canonical bucket dust and creator inboxes; this does not turn ongoing rebalancing into 1,000 NFT transactions. Keeper configuration/state now identifies `shared-pools-v2`; old state requires deliberate reconciliation without deleting pending transactions. Real signing and recovery still need staged network evidence.

The reorg-aware indexer, [confirmed API](FRONTEND_API.md) and optional [wallet/lifetime-claims replay](FRONTEND_HISTORY.md) are implemented but unhosted. The API pins deployment/helpers/runtime and EIP-1898 blocks; history requires actual NFT-creation coverage through the exact target. Missing history is unavailable, not zero. Gross fee/USD metrics remain open, and SQLite ingestion completeness remains an operator trust boundary. The public website is still simulated. Notifications/watcher exist but need real runtime/feed/signer staging and marketplace testing. The verified artwork bundle is prepared, **not uploaded or pinned**. Migration remains deliberate governance/operator work.

Published/indexed rewards, pending creator inboxes and funded canonical dust are settled for incumbents before entry. Unharvested venue fees and undelivered remote rewards retain collection/delivery-time collective membership; creator funds use processing-time membership. Separate royalty receipts use their activated rarity weights. Historical USD estimates are not funded liabilities or proof of entitlement.

The exact selected Robinhood native/Pons adapter is implemented as a candidate, with production admission still blocked by release gates. Target inputs are ZZZ, ETH, USDC and USDG. The immutable [Entry](ACTIVATION_ENTRY.md) now authenticates original-source value through an admitted typed route into WETH/ZZZ accounting; a quote or separate swap cannot substitute for this atomic binding. Actual route funding and fees must meet the whole-route 1% limit. USDC routing remains unavailable pending a funded reviewed route. The user reports creator sharing proportional to liquidity; [measurement policies](REVENUE_SHARE.md) are proposals, not approved fee entitlement or payments. Payout asset/frequency and final terms remain unset. Production VRF, bridge backing, authorities, deployment and independent audit remain open. Equivalent-asset migrations do not move the NFT or permit arbitrary conversion.
