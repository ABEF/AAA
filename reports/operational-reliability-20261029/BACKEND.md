## Implemented defects

1. **Indexer lost-depth commit.** `Indexer.step` admitted a chunk from the initial head only. An endpoint could later retain the exact tip hash while losing enough descendants; the chunk still became durable confirmed history. It now rereads a canonical head quantity after raw event validation, enforces the configured depth for the selected chunk tip, then rechecks that exact tip header before the transaction. A lower final head is accepted when the smaller selected chunk still has sufficient depth. A growing head does not enlarge the originally selected chunk or include pending logs. One extra read on an ingesting step; idle/reconcile read costs are unchanged.
2. **Indexer cursor ABA race.** A second legitimate Indexer could roll back/reingest the predecessor on another branch and restore the same next_block while the first prepared its batch. The cursor-only comparison missed that change and wrote discontinuous ancestry. The first now captures predecessor hash/parent/timestamp and compares the exact row under BEGIN IMMEDIATE with the cursor. On mismatch it preserves the second writer's committed truth and writes no stale batch. This does not authorize multiple operating writers or remove the existing process lock.
3. **Sold-out mint observation.** Unpaused operating mintedCount=1000 reported new_minting_stopped=false, while unsigned mint preparation correctly rejected the cap. Schema 2 now adds remaining_mint_supply=1000-minted and new_minting_stopped_by_supply; its aggregate stopped flag includes exhausted supply. Historical accounting1/current3/fresh4 keep their codec and pending randomness/reveal status. Remaining supply is not a mint-execution or VRF-liveness approval. No extra RPC read.
4. **Unobserved permanent contribution reservation.** Schema 6 observed bounded registered buckets but never read poolBucket, aliasBucket or bucketIdentity for the selected contribution pool. A fresh noncanonical empty reservation could be observation.complete=true and inventory_consistent=true even though first activation always reverts and the alias cannot be replaced. The current observer now records actual pool/alias/canonical IDs, target registration, bucket count and maximum at one required-canonical hash. The gate recomputes canonical identity from verified original lineage, ties registration/count to the complete existing holder inventory, verifies alias agreement and first-use capacity, and checks initialized current-pool identity. Old schema 6 inputs missing the new observation fail until recollected. Historical schema 5 is unchanged.

5. **Regressing indexed timestamps.** Hash-linked new headers were accepted even when their time decreased within a prepared chunk or below its retained predecessor. Existing reconcile compares a retained header against the same remote hash; it does not validate a new child's chronology. Step now enforces nondecreasing timestamps using the already-loaded predecessor tuple and previous new header. Equal L2 timestamps stay valid; a starting parent known only by configured hash supplies no invented time or extra RPC. Two pre-fix negative controls failed and two positive controls passed; affected 47 now pass.

The new observed `contribution_reservation` fields are exactly pool_id, bucket_id, alias_bucket_id, canonical_bucket_id, target_registered, bucket_count, maximum_buckets. Existing complete holder rows supply the exact current eight-word Position and physical LP evidence; no duplicate position copy is introduced.

The validator's `contribution_reservation_status` has available_by_reservation, reason and a partial-scope statement. It preserves active local/imported moved bucket A with current pool B, whose ID need not equal bucketIdentity(B). Existing canonical activation proof still must authenticate an imported successor. Parking/pause/canonical transitions/terminal settlement remain explicit lifecycle unavailability rather than invented initial-identity corruption. A destination must remain empty before its canonical header import; do not prescribe local successor pre-reservation. This status does not qualify prices/routes/gas/owner inputs or authorize a contribution.
