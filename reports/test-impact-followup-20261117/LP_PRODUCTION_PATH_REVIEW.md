## Important operational interaction retained and exercised

A large harvest can produce too much incumbent compound for the next contribution's idle ceiling. In the new controlled example there is6000 principal and600 designated compound. The entrant supplies2000; entrant-only placement would leave600/8600 idle, above the existing5% ceiling. **The contribution must revert**, including its temporary compound release, rather than deploy incumbent assets using entrant money or silently widen the ceiling.

The recovery sequence is ordinary keeper `Compounder.compound(bucket, minimumLiquidity, freshDeadline)` after its independent interval and readiness conditions. It deploys the existing300 ETH-unit+300 ZZZ-unit reserves into the **same main LP**, with no share issuance or rebalance-clock reset. The same incoming top-up can then be reviewed/retried under the original guards. If ordinary compounding is not ready because rebalance/range policy has priority, the operator must use the appropriate guarded rebalance/recovery flow; a contribution is not an automatic override.

The initial exploratory test that omitted this maintenance correctly failed `Slippage`. That raw result and exact source/artifact are retained; it is not counted as a passing sequence or misreported as stuck principal.
