## Findings and dispositions

**No confirmed new financial, authorization or scheduling defect was established in this partition.** Do not manufacture a source change or weaken a guard merely to produce a patch.

1. **Wall-clock sample liveness remains a qualification item.** A four-minute schedule leaves a one-minute maximum-gap margin. Serial receipt confirmation, slow RPC calls, budget refusal or optional work can consume that margin. Correct behavior is reset/refusal, not trading on expired evidence. Existing helper/unit/finite Ubuntu evidence does not prove a sustained production-provider SLA. Parent should retain Q06/P20/P21 and actual-provider qualification.
2. **First-time discovery has bounded fairness, not deadline priority.** Tracked windows get immediate priority. A previously unknown bucket reaches the ordinary scan after persistent optional-job rotations; active optional workers and their confirmation time can delay beginning the window. This is not loss of known evidence and is not an established defect. If a tighter first-enrollment SLA is required, specify it before reordering economic jobs.
3. **Software-only compounding policy is intentional.** An authorized keeper running different software could skip the extra waiting rule. User explicitly chose this trust boundary and governance can revoke the keeper key. Do not classify it as an unresolved implementation gap or move the rule on-chain without a new decision.
4. **Strict or conflicting controls can deliberately stop execution.** Gross loss, additional loss after actual directional fees, source agreement, source freshness, idle ceiling, main utilization and caller minimums protect different denominators/moments. Do not remove overlapping-looking checks without proving equivalence under rounding/callbacks/emergency retained bits.
5. **Diagnostics could display the conversion profitability allowance more directly.** `gas_review.maximum_cost_wei` consistently records the computed maximum transaction spend, not the caller profitability cap. The conversion log separately includes that cap. An additive, clearly named health allowance field is a possible usability enhancement, not a confirmed incorrect calculation or blocker.
6. **No automatic promise for uneconomic dust.** Classified fee dust, attributed idle and economically too-small conversion/buyback amounts have separate ownership/rules. They must not be collapsed into an administrator sweep or sold as royalties. Any gas-profitability policy addition is a separate decision; current asset/backing conservation holds by source.
