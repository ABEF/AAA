## Conclusion and implementation boundary

The current source manages main and optional residual as **one logical share
account with at most two Vault-owned physical LP NFTs in the same ETH/ZZZ pool**.
New composed tests confirm both-leg tracking through entry, growth, multiple
adjustments, harvest, partial claims, compound, market reversal and mode changes.
No production contract defect was reproduced in this campaign; all52 production
files remain unchanged. This does not establish correctness for every possible
input, dependency, market or live-service failure.

The work adds four focused contract journeys and current operating explanations.
It changes no authority, economic right, loss limit, policy default or deployed
state. Existing source-bound size/static evidence is reused, not rerun or claimed
as new. No service, website, Anvil, journal, signer or heartbeat changed.

## Activation and growth with two live legs

1. Resolve the current logical bucket/main/residual; validate owner, source,
   amount, policy, prices and deadline at execution. An old main ID is not the
   contribution destination supplied by the user.
2. Harvest/checkpoint incumbent fees and compound classifications before new
   shares or collective membership. The same actual portfolio is valued whether
   desired mode is on or off.
3. Balance and deploy only incoming funds into the **existing main** at its
   current range ratio. Do not rebuild the range or change residual L. Balancing
   swaps can still change market price and both legs' asset composition/value.
4. Reprice incumbents after the entry swap, prove marginal deployed LP and net
   added NAV, then issue shares of the aggregate bucket. Existing idle, residual
   or compound cannot subsidize the entrant's execution floor.
5. Activation adds one equal collective membership; growth adds none. Past funded
   rewards remain with incumbents. Once activated, the newcomer can earn later
   receipts, including fees generated by another user's later contribution.

Thus adding to the main does not give the new NFT exclusively main-position
rights: its shares participate in both legs at the reviewed net entry price.
60%/40% is a post-replacement limit, not a maintained per-NFT allocation.

## What happens as markets move

Residual geometry is contained within the main. An active residual with an
inactive main is not reachable under the current normal geometry. If price
exits the main, both are outside on the same asset side. A residual may otherwise
move from one-sided to mixed to the opposite asset while the main stays active.
It retains the same ID/L until a whole guarded adjustment or explicit close.

Repeated adjustments collect both legs, close both and rebuild from their actual
combined inventory plus attributed reserves/idle. Hybrid mode may create **one**
new useful residual; single mode creates none. Old associations clear after
successful burns. A failed close/swap/mint reverts the whole adjustment rather
than leaving half a portfolio. Nothing recursively creates unlimited residuals.

| Simulated macro-step | Desired mode | Required physical result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start after first hybrid adjustment | On | One main + one residual |
| Cycle0, price trend | On | Guarded rebuild; one main and at most one residual |
| Cycle1, reverse and disable | Off | Both old legs close; single active main |
| Cycle2, reverse and enable | On | New main + residual in the tested inventory |
| Cycle3, reverse with mode retained | On | Rebuild both; no third leg |
| Cycle4, reverse and disable | Off | Single active main |
| Cycle5, reverse and enable | On | New main + residual in the tested inventory |

The six cycles span at least six virtual days, with18 one-hour-spaced fee
collections, real Compounder calls, alternating ETH/ZZZ growth and partial claims.
This is deterministic virtual-time contract execution, not a live price backtest
or continuous multi-day keeper rehearsal. Macro-steps do not claim every hour
between them was serviced.

## Ordering, configuration and useful correction

Both serialized orderings pass: users entering/growing before a ready rebalance,
and rebalance before users. Contributions preserve stable geometry samples even
when main L changes; the next actual transaction resolves current state. The
campaign uses sequential caller-spoofed EVM calls at a single block/time for
these cases, not signed concurrent browser transactions or an RPC load test.

Three initial journeys rejected25USD growth. A detailed trace identifies the
older fixture's100USD `minDepositUSD`, stronger than ProtocolPolicy's5USD action
floor. This was valid rejection, not a broken activation or reward allocation.
The corrected fixture explicitly admits5USD, matching the fresh production
example; the final ordering tests retain a negative assertion that the stronger
100USD minimum rejects atomically before admitting the5USD flow. No production
guard was loosened. Effective minima must be read and explained by operator and
consumer; changing the generic action floor alone cannot lower a stronger pool
minimum.

## Long-term operating plan and remaining qualification

- Observe actual main/residual IDs, aggregate amounts, desired/applied revisions,
  idle, reserves, liabilities, price health and pending transactions. Desired
  mode is not a substitute for actual inventory. Never hide a residual on disable.
- Recover pending transactions first. Harvest is independent and hourly in source
  defaults; compound waits for eligible in-range/below-threshold/no-pending-policy
  conditions; ordinary adjustment retains cooldown, samples, jitter and financial
  gates. Direct test calls do not qualify service scheduling or jitter.
- Disable mode to request a guarded merge. Urgent residual-only close creates
  attributed idle and can block later entry under its idle ceiling. Reconcile and
  use documented guarded recovery, not a sweep or incoming users' funds.
- If markets/dependencies make an operation infeasible, retain backing and diagnose
  exact limits. The manual paused emergency remains an explicit governance choice
  with optional financial bypass and potentially near-total principal loss.
  Failed tokens or external venues can still prevent movement; no contract can
  guarantee recovery from arbitrary upstream failure.
- Qualify real dual feeds, venues/depth, gas,1000-holder populated scenarios,
  source6 API/site data, browser/RPC concurrency, reorgs, server/disk/key recovery,
  marketplace settlement and independent audit. Current older sites/Anvil cannot
  be relabelled source6. The [sole Todo](../../TODO.md) keeps those gates open.
