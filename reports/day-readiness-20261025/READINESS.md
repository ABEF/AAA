## Release order and residual trust

First close the stale static triage and unfinished OpenSea integration, while
selecting the real feed/route/VRF and authority inventory. Then qualify the
selected market, current-profile wallet/services, metadata and operating/gas
rehearsals. Freeze that complete release for final verification and independent
audit/remediation, and record named sign-off before seeking deployment approval.

No local test can guarantee market liquidity, permanent fee income, oracle
independence, an external custodian's honesty or recovery after chain halt.
Governance can immediately change allowed configuration and authorize trusted
custody. Per-operation loss caps do not bound market loss or cumulative turnover.
Those are explicit design/trust boundaries, not claims resolved by these passes.
