## What the cases prove

Both populations obey the real600Commun/300Rare/100Legendary stock. The811prefix uses600/210/1 and1023tickets; the999prefix uses599/300/100 and1499tickets, leaving one Commun NFT to activate. The smaller adversarial prefix costs more here, so maximum participant count alone does not establish worst gas. These are selected vectors, not a proof of the maximum over every cursor/tree state.

For every incumbent and every currency, independent quotient/remainder arithmetic over permanent one-based tickets equals the actual claimable amount; every pending unit is assigned to the earlier cohort. The entrant receives none of those older receipts. Healthy currencies must actually synchronize/enroll under the existing1M budget, not merely allow activation to return while silently deferring healthy assets. Pending/orphan/opening balances, liabilities and raw backing are checked separately.

The fault is deliberately limited to one optional currency, never the fixed LP asset. Its balance read exhausts its isolated1Mbudget; activation still enrolls15healthy currencies and keeps the faulted currency's old cohort. After explicit token recovery, the old receipt allocates before newcomer enrollment. A later full-weight receipt pays the newcomer only its new2units; actual current-owner balance deltas, final drained fault-currency backing/liabilities and zero repeat claims are verified. This controlled recovery is not a guarantee that every real token recovers.

The first-opening case materializes both the earlier openingPool and a pending receipt exactly at the cutoff; the late activation is excluded from those initial rights.
